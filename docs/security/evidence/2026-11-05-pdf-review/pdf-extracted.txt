1. EXECUTIVE THESIS: WHY CURVE-FITTING TODAY'S WINNERS FAILS
Over three consecutive observation cycles (Oct 30–Nov 2, Nov 1–Nov 4, and Nov 3–Nov 5, 2026), we investigated why top-gaining
Binance USD-M perpetual contracts frequently failed to appear in Atlas's published long candidates prior to or during their expansion
phases. A rigorous audit of both full-population market data (522  active USDT perpetuals) and stage-by-stage counterfactual pipeline
traces proves that reactive threshold tuning for yesterday's winners is an anti-pattern: today's leaders fail one gate (e.g., funding > +2.5
bps or trading below 4H EMA50), while tomorrow's leaders fail another (e.g., Stage 1 pre-enrichment absorption starvation or intraday 4-
hour scan cadence).
Core Strategic Diagnosis: Atlas currently conflates two fundamentally different quantitative objectives inside a single sequential filter: (1) Low-
Risk Coiled Entry Readiness (tight volatility compression above 4H EMA50 and 30d VAL with neutral/negative funding) and (2) Early Leader /
Regime-Shift Discovery (bottom-of-range reclaims, post-ignition continuations, and sudden volume/OI expansions). Furthermore, gating Stage 2
derivatives enrichment behind an un-enriched Stage 1 pass starves valid OI/funding setups before their derivative telemetry is ever fetched.

BOTTLENECK 1: PRE-GATE STRUCTURAL & FUNDING VETOES ELIMINATE 79.8% OF WINNERS BEFORE
GATES RUN
In CryptoOpportunityScreener._score_accumulation_setup  (crypto_screener.py:1028–1167 ) and classify_long_archetype
(archetypes.py:70–258 ), candidates must satisfy strict structural-support and funding conditions before evaluate_long_gates  is ever
reached. Across the 168 symbol-scans of the 14 Nov 4/5 winners, 134 scans (79.8%) were eliminated here:
Depressed / Bottom-of-Range Reclaim Leaders (63 / 168 scans): Every accumulation archetype requires close ≥ ema50_4h * 0.975
(or poc_30d * 0.975 ) and close ≥ val_30d . Tokens launching sharp reversals from oversold ranges below the 4H EMA50 or 30d VAL —
including JCTUSDT  (0.83x EMA50 ), FOGOUSDT  (0.80x EMA50 ), UAIUSDT  (0.44x EMA50 ), INITUSDT  (0.78x EMA50 ), BATUSDT  (0.88x
VAL ), and RAYSOLUSDT  (0.85x VAL ) — return None  immediately.
Hard Pre-Classification Funding Veto at +2.5 bps  (25 / 168 scans): Line 1048 (if funding is not None and funding >
Decimal("0.00025"): return None ) hard-vetoes any token whose 8h funding exceeds +2.5 bps  (0.025% ), even when funding is only
moderately positive during early trend accumulation. This single line rejected CAPUSDT  on 8/12 scans, CLANKERUSDT  on 5/12 scans,
ELSAUSDT  on 4/12 scans, and FLUIDUSDT  on 3/12 scans.
Post-Impulse Bandwidth Expansion (26 / 168 scans): Requiring bb_bandwidth_4h ≤ 0.22  or bb_inside_kc_4h  excludes multi-day
continuation runners (AIOUSDT , FLUIDUSDT , CLANKERUSDT , LYNUSDT ) once their initial 4H impulse expands Bollinger bandwidth above
0.22 .

else None , ignoring quote.funding_rate  (already fetched in Stage 0
via bulk premiumIndex ). And oi_change_7d  is None  in Stage 1. Thus
OI_ACCUMULATION_LONG , SHORT_SQUEEZE_ABSORPTION_LONG , and
FUNDING_SQUEEZE_SPRING_LONG cannot match in Stage 1.

BOTTLENECK 3: 4-HOUR SCHEDULED SCAN CADENCE VS. INTRADAY IGNITIONS
With scans running every 4 hours (00:01, 04:01, 08:01, 12:01, 16:01, 20:01 UTC ), explosive transitions that occur shortly after a scan
boundary complete their move before the next scan. On November 5, FOGOUSDT  triggered a 30.31x  volume 5m breakout at 04:05 UTC  (4
minutes after the 04:01 UTC  scan) and entered the top-10 leaderboard by 05:00 UTC  — 3 hours before the 08:01 UTC  scan. JCTUSDT
(11:25 UTC ) and LYNUSDT  (02:15 UTC ) similarly ignited between 4-hour boundaries.
